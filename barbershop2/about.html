Meet Zaccheaus

Zaccheaus Unisex Salon & Spa — also known as The Prolific Unisex Salon — was built on a simple idea: every client deserves the best seat in the chair. From precision haircuts and dreadlocks to braids, installations, nails and full spa care, we offer a complete grooming experience under one roof in Ogbomosho. And when you can't come to us, our mobile service brings the salon to your doorstep. We also train the next generation of barbers and stylists — sharing the craft we've spent years perfecting.
